Ironwood Summit

A strenuous six-mile out-and-back climbing eleven hundred feet to the highest point in the park network.

Distance and elevation

6.2 miles round trip, 1,100 feet of elevation gain, typically three to four hours.

Trailhead

Park at the Ironwood trailhead lot off Summit Road. The lot fills early on weekends.

Current conditions

Dry and clear as of this week. The final quarter mile is exposed rock scrambling.
