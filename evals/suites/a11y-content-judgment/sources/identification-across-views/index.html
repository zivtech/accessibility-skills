Get outside at Alderbrook

Alderbrook Parks & Trails maintains eighteen miles of hiking trails across six trailheads, from easy loop walks to a full-day ridge crossing.

Plan a hike

Browse the trail directory for distance, difficulty, and current conditions, or open the interactive map to see everything at once.

Before you go

Cell coverage is spotty past the second overlook. Download a trail map in advance and tell someone your planned route.
