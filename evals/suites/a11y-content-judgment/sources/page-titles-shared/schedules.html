Bus schedules

Weekday and weekend departure times for every route are published here on the first of each month.

Weekday service

Buses depart the harbor terminal every ten minutes from six in the morning until seven at night, then every twenty minutes until midnight.

Weekend service

Saturday and Sunday buses run every thirty minutes from seven in the morning until midnight.

Printable timetables

A printable timetable for each route is available at the harbor terminal information counter.
