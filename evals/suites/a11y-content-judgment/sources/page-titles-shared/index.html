Get around town on Harborline Transit

Harborline Transit operates twelve bus routes connecting the harbor district, the university, and the outlying neighborhoods. Buses run every ten to twenty minutes on weekdays and every thirty minutes on weekends.

Plan your trip

Use the routes page to find a bus near you, or check the schedules page for departure times at your stop.

Service alerts

Detours and delays are posted as soon as they are known. Check the alerts page before you head out during storms or major events.

Fares

A single ride costs two dollars and fifty cents. Weekly and monthly passes are available at the harbor terminal ticket window and through the mobile app.
