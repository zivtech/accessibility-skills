Routes

Harborline Transit runs twelve numbered routes across the city. Select a route below to see its full path and stops.

  • Route 101 — Harbor Loop
  • Route 118 — University Express
  • Route 142 — Riverside Local
  • Route 204 — Crosstown Limited
  • Route 219 — Northgate Shuttle

Accessible vehicles

Every bus in the fleet is equipped with a low floor and a fold-out ramp. Priority seating is available near the front doors.

Bike racks

Each bus carries a two-bike rack on the front bumper at no extra charge.
