Route 204 — Crosstown Limited

Route 204 connects the harbor terminal to the university campus with limited stops, cutting the trip to eighteen minutes at peak times.

Stops

Harbor Terminal, Fifth and Main, University Gate, Campus Center.

Frequency

Buses run every fifteen minutes on weekdays and are not in service on weekends.

Fleet

Route 204 is served by the newer low-floor articulated buses with extra standing room for the campus commute.
