## Per-operation reasoning

Package-level note first: four of the five records are sign-off prose, and one is a fully-fielded YAML record. The five rules are applied to each operation independently, and evidence recorded for one operation is never borrowed by another — that is what rule 2 means by *the same session* and *the setup that immediately preceded it*. Rules 1 (`bounded_diagnostic_not_promoted`) and 3 (`natural_only_conditional_state`) do not engage anywhere in this package: there is no `focus_stagnation_observed`-class note being promoted to a trap conclusion, and no conditional state (empty result, error) is claimed or induced. I say that explicitly rather than silently omitting it, so the empty entries are a judgment and not an oversight.

### OP-ESC — dismiss the Details dialog with Escape

- **Rule 2 (`setup_action_continuity`) — satisfied.** `session: 2026-09-02-JR-a` is stated. `before.reached_by` describes the setup (load results view → reach the 'Renew' row action → Enter to open the Details dialog) and `before.locus` states its terminal identity ("Details dialog open; focus on its heading 'Item details'"). The action (`Escape`) starts from that identity in that session. Setup terminus and action start are the same locus; the chain is continuous and self-contained.
- **Rule 4 (`passive_observation_binding`) — satisfied.** `observed` is an actual result of the causing key press, not a state read off the tree: "Dialog closed; NVDA announced 'Renew, button'; visible focus ring on that button." `observed_via: announcement` binds it. The announcement string is quoted, so there is a result to bind — this is the distinction that OP-LABEL fails.
- **Rule 5 (`ancestor_remapping_review`) — satisfied.** `target_reached: exact`. The trigger is on the focus path; no ancestor stands in for it.
- **Rules 1, 3 — do not engage.**

No rule broken. Admitted, and the evidence decides the operation's own predicate: focus returned to the invoking trigger after Escape. **PASS.** Calibration on what that PASS is worth: under the human lane, rules 2 and 4 are structured self-report, not a verified session identity or a source allowlist. That is a known property of the mechanism, not a defect in this record, and the compensating control is a second confirmation — not something this package can supply and not something the five rules require of it. The boundary below says so.

### OP-RETURN — focus return to the Renew dialog trigger

The whole record is "Checked the Renew dialog; focus return works fine now — J.R."

- **Rule 2 (`setup_action_continuity`) — VIOLATED.** No `session`, no `before.reached_by`, no `before.locus`. There is no starting identity to compare against any setup terminus, so the rule's predicate cannot be shown at all. This is the canonical "I checked it, it's fine" shape.
- **Rule 4 (`passive_observation_binding`) — VIOLATED.** "works fine now" is a conclusion, not an observed result, and there is no stated action for it to bind to and no `observed_via`. A standalone assertion about focus return is exactly what rule 4 refuses: focus behaviour is established by the causing key press and its observed result.
- **Rule 5 — does not engage.** Nothing indicates the trigger was off the focus path, so there is no ancestor substitution to review. I am deliberately not stacking rule 5 on top of a missing `target_reached` where no reachability problem is in evidence.

The obvious temptation here is to let OP-ESC carry OP-RETURN: OP-ESC's admitted `observed` literally records focus landing on "Renew, button". I decline. First, that is a different operation and a different remediation item, and the operations were planned separately. Second, OP-ESC's evidence is about dismissing the **Details** dialog; OP-RETURN is about the **Renew** dialog's own trigger. Third, rule 2 is per-operation by construction — a walk that observed one thing does not thereby observe another. Rejected evidence never moves an operation, and this operation has no admitted observation and no prior disposition stated in the package. **UNTESTED.**

### OP-AD — audio description track for the training video

The record is "The player's CC/AD menu lists a 'Described' track… Nothing was played and nothing was heard."

- **Rules 2 and 5 — do not apply.** This is the attended-media shape: an observation about content rather than about an operation, with no locus and no action. Applying continuity or ancestor-remapping here would be inventing a defect. The binding field for this shape is `played` — the stretches actually played, with the alternative selected.
- **Rule 4 (`passive_observation_binding`) — VIOLATED.** `played` is empty; nothing was played and nothing was heard. What is offered instead is a menu listing — the presence of a track label in the player UI. That is precisely the case rule 4 is written to catch: a listing or a track label offered as evidence of the alternative. A menu entry establishes that a track is *declared*, never that it exists, is synchronised, is audible, or describes the visual content.
- **Rules 1, 3 — do not engage.**

Disposition requires care here, because BLOCKED is the attractive wrong answer. BLOCKED needs an **admitted** bounded observation about the operation standing without the instrument reading that would decide it — the person's "I watched the animation and cannot count flashes per second" case, where something was genuinely attended and the gap is the instrument. Here nothing was attended: the sole observation is inadmissible under rule 4, and rejected evidence never moves an operation. Nothing admitted bears on the operation. **UNTESTED**, not BLOCKED.

### OP-OPTION — nested option state inside the filter combobox

The record is "Tabbed to the filter and the highlighted option's selected state looked right — J.R."

- **Rule 5 (`ancestor_remapping_review`) — VIOLATED.** This is the load-bearing failure. Tab reaches the combobox — the composite's owner — not the nested option, which is not on the focus path. Evidence recorded at the owner is being read as evidence about the descendant with no reviewed, separately frozen owner/descendant mapping cited, and the package itself confirms none is cited. That is a silent nearest-ancestor substitution: it is not evidence about the target.
- **Rule 2 (`setup_action_continuity`) — VIOLATED.** An action is named ("Tabbed to the filter") but there is no `session` and no `before` — no starting locus, no setup terminus to match it against. A package missing those fields fails the rule that reads them; rule 2's predicate cannot be shown. Note the contrast with OP-AD, where rule 2 is carved out by shape; this operation *is* an operation with a locus and an action, so the rule applies and the fields are simply absent.
- **Rule 4 (`passive_observation_binding`) — VIOLATED.** "the highlighted option's selected state looked right" is a state read off the rendered UI, with no `observed_via` and no key press that caused the option's highlight (no arrow-key navigation is recorded — only a Tab that lands on the owner). It is a passive state observation offered standalone, and "looked right" is a conclusion rather than an observed result. If a fully-fielded record breaks rule 4 on `observed: "As expected"` (OP-LABEL, below), an unfielded "looked right" breaks it a fortiori.

Three rules, one record — I have checked that this is not over-reading. Each is a distinct missing predicate (identity/continuity, bound result, target identity), not one defect counted three times, and I have withheld rule 5 from OP-RETURN where the same field is also missing but no reachability problem is in evidence. **UNTESTED.**

### OP-LABEL — accessible name of the row-action button

- **Rule 2 (`setup_action_continuity`) — satisfied.** Same `session: 2026-09-02-JR-a`; `before.reached_by` gives a self-contained setup from a fresh load of the results view and `before.locus` its terminus ("Focus on the first row-action control, before the 'Renew' control"); the `Tab` action starts there. It is also internally consistent with OP-ESC's chain (Tab×4 lands before 'Renew', Tab×5 on it). Continuity holds.
- **Rule 5 (`ancestor_remapping_review`) — satisfied.** `target_reached: exact`; the button is on the focus path.
- **Rule 4 (`passive_observation_binding`) — VIOLATED, and alone.** Every field is filled, including `observed_via: announcement` — but `observed: "As expected"` records no result. The `expected` field says NVDA should announce "Renew, button"; the `observed` field does not say what NVDA actually announced. `observed_via` names a channel with nothing carried on it. This is the tidy-package failure: the form is complete and the content is empty, so there is no observed result of the action for the self-report to bind to. Nothing here establishes the accessible name — a filled template is not an observation.
- **Rules 1, 3 — do not engage.**

Two things I explicitly decline to treat as findings, because no sixth rule is needed and none is available. (a) The pre-counted Tab sequences ("Tab x4", "Tab x5") violate the skill's keyboard-driving discipline — snapshot and act on what is actually focused, never send a counted burst — but that is a driving-practice rule for interactive modes, not one of the five admissibility rules, and it is not a basis for rejecting evidence here. (b) The `closes: rem-renew-button-name-51e0c9a7` reference implies this operation previously carried an adverse finding. Rejected evidence never moves an operation, and a recorded prior state would stay — but the package states no prior disposition value and says nothing else about this engagement exists, so I will not manufacture a FAIL from the existence of a remediation id. **UNTESTED**, with the still-open remediation item named in the boundary. This is where my confidence is lowest in the whole review; if the campaign record elsewhere carries a prior FAIL for `rem-renew-button-name-51e0c9a7`, that prior FAIL stands and this row should read FAIL instead.

### Package-level admissibility

`rules_violated` is non-empty, so the package is REJECT. That is a verdict on the evidence, not on the component: nothing here says the Renew dialog, the audio description, the combobox option, or the button name are inaccessible. Four of five claimed PASSes are unsupported, which is a different statement from "four of five are broken".
